# Crowdfunding Strategy — Omer Gozlan 2026

## Goal
Raise **$50-75K USD** from the Jewish diaspora (N. America + S. America + Central America) to fund 2026 Pro Tour season (CEV Bulgaria, Volleyball World England, China x2 Futures, U18+U20 European + World Championships).

## Why this works
- Jewish diaspora is the **most loyal donor pool** for Israeli individuals, not just causes
- Beach volleyball = under-funded Olympic sport → "underdog Israeli athlete" frames clean
- Diaspora donors **expect** Israeli-flag-abroad narrative — already convinced before pitch
- Multi-tour calendar = recurring content for follow-up campaigns Year 2/3

## Vehicle stack
| Layer | Tool | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Primary crowdfunding | **Charidy** (charidy.com) | Matching-grant 24-36hr format. Strong in Orthodox + diaspora. Israeli/Jewish framing built-in. |
| Backup public | **GoFundMe** | Catches non-Jewish + secular donors, casual share-virality |
| Tax vehicle (US) | **PEF Israel Endowment Funds** *or* **Maccabi USA host** | 501(c)(3) required for tax-deductible US donations |
| Tax vehicle (Canada) | Canadian Friends of Israeli sport org TBD | CRA charitable receipt |

## Realistic split of $50K
- **30% lead matching donors** (5-10 × $2-10K) — pre-campaign warm network
- **50% federation + Maccabi community network** — emailed list of ~5K people
- **20% social media tail** — Charidy + IG amplification

## Timeline (8 weeks pre-launch)
| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Decide tax vehicle (PEF or Maccabi USA host). Open Charidy page draft. |
| 2 | Recruit 5-10 lead matching donors from warm network (target $20-30K matched) |
| 3 | Film 2-3 min campaign video (training + interview + ask) |
| 4 | Build Charidy page (story, photos, video, perks). Mirror on GoFundMe. |
| 5 | Federation outreach blast (en/es/pt). Maccabi chapter outreach. |
| 6 | Media pitch wave (JTA, Jewish Insider, Aurora, ENLACE Judío) |
| 7 | Pre-launch warm reminders to lead donors + community list. Press placements lock. |
| 8 | **LAUNCH 36-hour Charidy match-a-thon** + Instagram live + livestream |

## Required assets
- 2-3 min campaign video (HE captions + ES subs)
- 8-10 high-res photos (medals, training, family, Wingate)
- Story copy: 600-word EN, 500-word HE, 500-word ES
- Lead donor matching commitments (paper trail)
- Tax-vehicle confirmation letter
- Bank wiring instructions
- Press one-pager (separate from sponsor media-kit — different angle)

## Pre-launch must-haves
1. **Tax-vehicle host** confirmed (PEF or Maccabi USA)
2. **5+ lead donors** with signed matching commitments (total ≥$15K)
3. **Press placement** locked at JTA or Jewish Insider for launch week
4. **Maccabi USA endorsement** (warm letter or interview)

## Risks
- No tax-vehicle = US take collapses 60-70%
- No lead matchers = no "match-a-thon" magic
- Concurrent geopolitical event (Israel news cycle) can swamp campaign — have a 2-week flexible launch window
- Charidy fee (typically 5-7%) + payment processing 3% = ~10% off top
