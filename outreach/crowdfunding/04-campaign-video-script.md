# Campaign Video Script — 2:30 min

**Format**: Vertical 9:16 (Instagram/Charidy mobile) + 16:9 cut (desktop/embed)
**Languages**: HE primary audio · EN subtitles burned-in · ES subtitles toggleable
**Production budget**: ₪3-8K (1 day shoot at Wingate + 1 beach location + editor 2 days)
**Music**: licensed track, beach + cinematic build (not generic stock)

---

## STRUCTURE (six beats, 2:30 total)

### Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15)
**Visual**: Slow-motion of Omer spiking on sand at sunset. No words yet.
**Audio**: Wave + crowd-cheer build. Single beat-drop on the spike.
**On-screen text (EN)**: "17 years old. #1 in Israel. Headed for the world."

### Beat 2 — Identity (0:15–0:45)
**Visual**: Omer talking direct-to-camera, sitting on beach in Israel team kit. Cuts to training reps, medal photos, family.
**Audio (Omer, HE)**:
"שמי עומר גוזלן. אני בן 17. אני שחקן כדורעף חופים. אני מספר 1 בישראל ב-U18 וגם ב-U20. השנה אני נכנס לסבב הבינלאומי הבכיר."

**EN subtitles**:
"My name is Omer Gozlan. I'm 17. I play beach volleyball. I'm ranked #1 in Israel in both U18 and U20. This year I'm stepping into the senior international tour."

### Beat 3 — The Stakes (0:45–1:15)
**Visual**: Map animation showing the 2026 calendar — pins drop on Bulgaria, England, China x2, Italy, Turkey, Netherlands, Hungary, Israel. Maccabi-style Star-of-David overlay subtle in corner.
**Audio (dad voiceover OR Eyal Aharonson, HE)**:
"שש מדינות. שמונה תחרויות. מסלול אולימפי. בענף אולימפי בלי משכורת פדרציה, בלי תקציב, בלי חסות מובילה — אנחנו צריכים את הקהילה."

**EN subtitles**:
"Six countries. Eight tournaments. An Olympic pathway. In an Olympic sport with no federation salary, no budget, no anchor sponsor — we need the community."

### Beat 4 — Authority (1:15–1:45)
**Visual**: Eyal Aharonson on camera at Wingate (or his existing quote frame). Cuts to Wingate Academy exterior + Omer training with national team.
**Audio (Aharonson, HE)**:
"מעולם לא היה תלמיד י"א עם שתי מדליות אירופאיות. עומר הוא כישרון יוצא דופן. אם נגבה אותו עכשיו — הוא יגיע לפסגה."

**EN subtitles**:
"There has never been an 11th-grade student with two European medals. Omer is an exceptional talent. If we back him now — he will reach the top."

### Beat 5 — The Ask (1:45–2:15)
**Visual**: Back to Omer, direct-to-camera, beach behind him. Hands open.
**Audio (Omer, HE switching mid-line to EN for diaspora ears)**:
"בקמפיין הזה כל דולר מוכפל. אני מבקש מהקהילה היהודית בכל מקום בעולם — צפון, מרכז, ודרום אמריקה — לעמוד מאחורי. *Help me get to every court. Help me carry the flag.*"

**EN subtitles**:
"In this campaign, every dollar is matched. I'm asking the Jewish community everywhere — North, Central, and South America — to stand behind me. Help me get to every court. Help me carry the flag."

### Beat 6 — CTA + Brand (2:15–2:30)
**Visual**: URL + Charidy logo + dates + match-a-thon countdown.
**On-screen text (EN)**:
> **36 HOURS · EVERY DOLLAR MATCHED**
> **CHARIDY.COM/[campaign-slug]**
> **[Launch Date]**

**Audio**: Music swell, no voice. Final freeze on Omer's face.
