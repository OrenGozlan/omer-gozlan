# Matching Donor Pitch — Warm Network

**Target**: 10-15 warm contacts who could pledge **$2K-$10K** each as matching donors
**Why critical**: Without matching donors, no "match-a-thon" magic. Lead pool of $15-25K → community matches it 1:1 or 2:1 → final $50-75K. With zero lead matchers, campaign caps at ~$5K.
**Who to target first**:
- Family + extended family
- Wingate Academy alumni who became successful
- Eyal Aharonson's network
- Friends-of-the-family wealthy individuals in IL + diaspora
- Family physicians, lawyers, accountants
- Synagogue community leaders
- Israeli-American Council (IAC) chapter chairs (US/Canada/LatAm)

**Channel**: Personal phone call > WhatsApp voice note > personal email. NEVER mass email this ask.
**Timing**: 6 weeks before launch

## EN VERSION (personal phone-call script + email follow-up)

### Phone call opener (45 seconds)
"Hi [Name], it's Oren. Quick one — I'm calling about Omer. You know he's been competing in beach volleyball — well, this year he's gone to a different level. He's now ranked #1 in Israel in both U18 and U20, and starting in 2026 he'll be on the senior international Pro Tour. Bulgaria, England, China — six countries this year, plus European and World Championships.

The catch is — federation salary doesn't exist at this level for an Olympic sport like this. We're funding it ourselves, plus sponsors we're building. And we're running a crowdfunding campaign with the Jewish diaspora — Charidy match-a-thon. To make it work, we need a small group of lead donors who'll pledge to match incoming donations 1-to-1.

That's why I'm calling. Would you consider being one of the lead matching donors? Ballpark — anywhere from $2,000 to $10,000, your call. You commit to matching that amount during the campaign, your name on the donor wall, and your pledge unlocks 5-10x that in community donations.

No pressure today — I just wanted to ask before we lock the campaign date."

### Email follow-up (after the call, day-of)

**Subject:** Omer's 2026 campaign — the lead-donor ask in one page

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the call. As promised, the one-pager. Two things to look at:
1. Athlete profile + 2026 schedule: https://orengozlan.github.io/omer-gozlan
2. 1-page sponsor brief: https://orengozlan.github.io/omer-gozlan/media-kit.html

The ask in plain language:
- **Campaign**: 36-hour Charidy match-a-thon, launching [date], targeting Jewish diaspora across the Americas
- **Lead donor commitment**: pledge $X (you tell me). During the campaign, every dollar a community donor gives is matched by lead-donor pledges 1-to-1 until your pledge is exhausted.
- **What you actually pay**: only what gets matched — never more than your pledge. Funds go through a US 501(c)(3) host (tax-deductible).
- **What we ask in return**: your name on the donor wall (or anonymous if you prefer), and — if you want — a 15-second video clip for the campaign page where you say one sentence about why you're backing Omer.

Goal: 5-10 lead donors totaling $15-25K. With that, we can credibly target $50-75K total raise.

If you're in, just reply "in for $X" and I'll send the formal pledge form. If you want to think about it, take a week — I'll follow up.

Thank you for even considering this. Means everything at this stage.

Oren
